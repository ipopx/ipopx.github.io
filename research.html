Research

  1. Efficient and Minimax Optimal In-context Nonparametric Regression with Transformers

    Michelle Ching, Ioana Popescu, Nico Smith · Supervised by Prof. Richard Samworth, Tianyi Ma, and Dr. William Underwood (University of Cambridge)

    ICML 2026 arXiv ↗
    • Studied the expressivity of transformers for in-context learning and showed that a pretrained transformer with few parameters and pretraining sequences is minimax-optimal for ICL regression.

  2. Reconstruction of 3D Spatial Transcriptomics Volumes

    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Valentina Boeva and Lovro Rabuzin (ETH Zurich)

    Research Project Feb 2026 – Jun 2026
    • Developed a generative model based on unbalanced optimal transport and thin-plate splines to reconstruct missing slices in 3D spatial transcriptomics volumes, and evaluated it against interpolation-based and flow-matching baselines.

  3. Energy of Superlinear Preferential Attachment Trees

    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Igor Kortchemski (ENS Ulm)

    Bachelor Thesis · 2025 PDF ↗ prize mention ↗
    • Analyzed the asymptotic energy of superlinear preferential attachment trees; extended known results for linear and sublinear models by characterizing distinct asymptotic energy regimes.
    • Special mention from the jury of the Junior Maryam Mirzakhani Prize 2025.
  4. ∫dU

    Weingarten Calculus

    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Guillaume Dubach (École Polytechnique)

    Research Project · 2024 PDF ↗
    • Surveyed Weingarten Calculus for undergraduates, covering random matrix theory, representation theory of compact groups, and algebraic combinatorics.

Selected Projects

    Open LLM Benchmark on ETH Math Exams

    Coding Project Sep 2026 – Dec 2026
    • Developing a benchmark to evaluate open-weight LLMs (Kimi, Gemma, GLM, Mistral-Small, Apertus) on ETH math exams from over 30 courses, investigating baseline performance and the impact of agentic components (tool use, self-check, skills, and multi-agent systems) in mitigating failure modes.

  2. Evolution Simulator

    Coding Project Nov 2023 – Jan 2024 code ↗
    • Built a biological simulation in C++ where autonomous creatures with evolving bodies and brains compete for food and survival.
    • Used NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies) to evolve each creature's neural network under natural-selection pressure.
