About

I am a second-year Master's student in Data Science at ETH Zurich and an ESOP Excellence Scholar. Before that, I completed a dual degree in Mathematics and Computer Science at École Polytechnique in Paris, where I finished valedictorian.

I previously interned as a Software Engineer at Microsoft, and this summer as a Quantitative Research Intern at Optiver. I am looking for PhD positions in ML for biomedicine, and for opportunities in the biotech industry.

I am interested in diffusion and flow matching, transformers and foundation models, and machine learning for genomics and medicine.
