About

I am Ioana Popescu, a first-year Master's student in Data Science at ETH Zurich. My interests lie broadly in optimization, probability, and statistics, with a recent focus on applications to genomics. Before that, I completed a dual degree in Mathematics and Computer Science at École Polytechnique in Paris, where I finished valedictorian.

My recent work spans three directions:

  • ML for genomics. At ETH Zurich I am developing a generative model to reconstruct missing slices in 3D spatial transcriptomics volumes, supervised by Prof. Valentina Boeva and Lovro Rabuzin.
  • Statistical learning theory. At the University of Cambridge I studied in-context learning in transformers through nonparametric regression, culminating in an arXiv preprint with Prof. Richard Samworth's group.
  • Probability on graphs. For my bachelor thesis at ENS Ulm with Prof. Igor Kortchemski, I analyzed the asymptotic energy of preferential attachment trees, receiving a special mention from the jury of the Junior Maryam Mirzakhani Prize 2025.

I previously interned as a Software Engineer at Microsoft, and I will be joining Optiver this summer as a Quantitative Research Intern. I am still exploring plans for next year, and I am considering both PhD programs—particularly in probability or machine learning for genomics—and opportunities in industry.

Outside of academics, I enjoy reading French literature, playing video games, cooking, and pilates.

Research

  1. Reconstruction of 3D Spatial Transcriptomics Volumes
    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Valentina Boeva and Lovro Rabuzin (ETH Zurich)
    Ongoing · Feb 2026 – Jun 2026
    • Developing a generative model to reconstruct missing slices in 3D spatial transcriptomics volumes, preserving biologically meaningful cell-type structure, gene expression patterns, and spatial cellular organization.
  2. Efficient and Minimax Optimal In-context Nonparametric Regression with Transformers
    Michelle Ching, Ioana Popescu, Nico Smith, Tianyi Ma, William G. Underwood, Richard J. Samworth
    Preprint · 2026 · arXiv
    • Studied in-context learning in transformers through nonparametric regression; showed a transformer with appropriately constructed weights can simulate the local polynomial estimator, and proved matching convergence rates via empirical process theory.
    • Supervised by Prof. Richard Samworth, Dr. William Underwood, and Tianyi Ma (University of Cambridge).
  3. Energy of Random Trees
    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Igor Kortchemski (ENS Ulm)
    Bachelor Thesis · 2025 · PDF · prize mention
    • Analyzed the asymptotic energy of superlinear preferential attachment trees; extended known results for linear and sublinear models by characterizing distinct asymptotic energy regimes.
    • Special mention from the jury of the Junior Maryam Mirzakhani Prize 2025.
  4. Weingarten Calculus
    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Guillaume Dubach (École Polytechnique)
    Research Project · 2024 · PDF
    • Surveyed Weingarten Calculus for undergraduates, covering random matrix theory, representation theory of compact groups, and algebraic combinatorics.
  5. p-adic Numbers
    Ioana Popescu · Supervised by Prof. Frank Pacard (École Polytechnique)
    Seminar · Mar 2023
    • Seminar introducing the construction of p-adic numbers, with proofs of Hensel's Lemma and Ostrowski's Theorem.
